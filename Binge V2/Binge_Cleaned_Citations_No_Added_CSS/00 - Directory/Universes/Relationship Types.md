# ◇ RELATIONSHIP TYPES

> [!abstract]- Main
> The primary work in a continuity.

> [!abstract]- Spin-off
> A work derived from an existing series, usually following a different character, location or premise.

> [!abstract]- Sequel
> Continues a previous story after its events.

> [!abstract]- Prequel
> Takes place before an existing story.

> [!abstract]- Animated adaptation
> An animated version/adaptation of an existing property. It is not automatically the same continuity.

> [!abstract]- Companion
> Supplementary programming connected to the main work without necessarily being a conventional spin-off.

> [!abstract]- Alternate perspective
> Retells, overlaps or expands events from another character's point of view.

> [!abstract]- Alternate universe
> A distinct continuity or reality within the broader franchise.

> [!abstract]- Reimagining
> A substantially reworked interpretation of the property.

> [!abstract]- Backdoor pilot
> A story introduced inside another series to establish a proposed spin-off.

> [!abstract]- International adaptation
> A separately produced local version. It should not be treated as the same continuity unless evidence supports that.

> [!abstract]- Crossover
> A story that deliberately crosses into another series/continuity.

> [!abstract]- Related
> Franchise or production relationship without enough evidence to call it the same continuity.
