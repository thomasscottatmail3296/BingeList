# ◇ VIEWING PROVIDERS — AUSTRALIA

Central provider registry for the Binge Vault. Prices below are Australian consumer prices verified from provider-owned pages during Batch 100. Availability remains title-specific and can change.

◇ PRICING / BILLING STANDARD
- Currency: AUD
- Billing occurrence is recorded explicitly.
- Promotional introductory pricing is not treated as the normal ongoing price.
- Every verified price has an `as of` date and source note.
- Where an official page did not expose an Australian price clearly enough to verify, the entry remains `unverified`.
