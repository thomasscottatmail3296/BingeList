# Classic Who — Season 05

◇ STRUCTURE

- 7 serials, 40 broadcast episodes.
- 22 episodes survive in the BBC archive; 18 remain missing.
- Missing archive status is historical preservation metadata; it does not imply the episode was never broadcast.
- Animated reconstructions exist for missing episodes from The Abominable Snowmen, The Ice Warriors, The Web of Fear, and Fury from the Deep.
