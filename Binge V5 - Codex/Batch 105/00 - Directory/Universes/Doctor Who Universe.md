## CONTINUITY POLICY

The vault uses **Whoniverse** as the umbrella relationship, not as a claim that every production shares one simple uninterrupted continuity.

- Classic Who and New Who are linked as the same long-running television programme across eras.
- Torchwood, The Sarah Jane Adventures and Class are separate series with spin-off relationships.
- Tales of the TARDIS is treated as a companion/archive production rather than a normal Doctor Who season.
- The War Between the Land and the Sea is a modern UNIT-focused spin-off.
- K-9 is kept separate from the BBC television continuity because it was produced outside the main BBC Doctor Who production line.
- Animated, webcast, charity and parody productions are separate nodes with explicit relationship labels.
