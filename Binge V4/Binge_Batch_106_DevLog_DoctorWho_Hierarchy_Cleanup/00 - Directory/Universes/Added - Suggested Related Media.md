## DC

The Arrowverse is a major missing relationship layer if the intention is to capture the wider DC television universe. It includes Arrow, The Flash, Supergirl, Legends of Tomorrow, Black Lightning, Batwoman, plus animated/web material such as Vixen and Freedom Fighters: The Ray and crossover material.
