## ◇ Watch-status vocabulary

The canonical watch-status values are exactly:

1. `Not Watched`
2. `Watched`
3. `In Progress of Watching`

Future/unreleased episodes use `Not Watched` plus `availability_state: "upcoming"`; this keeps the watch-status dropdown strictly limited to the three user-facing values.
